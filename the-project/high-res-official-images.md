# Official images

These files are the current promo images and product screenshots kept in the repository for press, linking, embedding, and general project references. They are the images to use when you need something to pair with a Patcher mention outside the app.

## What is included

Three promo images and seven current product screenshots covering the main surfaces users see in the app:

- home, modules, racks, patches
- user area, account settings, public profile

The screenshots reflect the current product; the promo images are stylized brand renders. Use whichever fits the context.
